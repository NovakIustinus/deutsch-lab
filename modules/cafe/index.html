5 · How it works
Ordering = Akkusativ. Ich hätte gern einen Cappuccino (der) · Ich hätte gern eine heiße Schokolade (die) · Ich hätte gern ein Omelette (das). Only der → einen changes.
10 questions, 4 points each (40 total), mostly tapping: pick the English for a customer’s order · tap the words in order to build what you say (two “you ask”, one “you answer”, one order, one price) · hear a price and pick it · pick einen/eine/ein · pick a menu word’s meaning · plus one written answer. First try 4 pts, second 2, third 1. In the written answer, words you got right turn green and stay put — you only fix the red/missing ones. A clue appears after 3 wrong tries; after 5 you can give up and the answer is shown. Capitals and punctuation don’t matter; ä/ö/ü/ß can be typed ae/oe/ue/ss.
